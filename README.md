# ✊✋✌️ Rock Paper Scissors

Hey! Welcome to my Rock Paper Scissors game — a fun little project I built to sharpen my frontend skills. It's simple, it works, and honestly... it's kind of addictive.

---

## 🎮 What is this?

It's the classic **Rock Paper Scissors** game you already know — but playable right in your browser. No downloads, no sign-ups, no nonsense. Just you vs. the computer.

Pick your move. See if you can outsmart a random number generator. (Spoiler: sometimes you can't. That's life.)

---

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Click **Rock**, **Paper**, or **Scissors**
3. Watch the computer make its choice
4. See who wins!
5. Repeat until you either win 10 times or lose your mind

---

## 🗂️ What's Inside

```
📁 root
 ├── index.html   → The structure (where the game lives)
 ├── script.js    → The brains (game logic, score tracking)
 └── style.css    → The looks (makes it not ugly)
```

## 🛠️ Built With

Just the basics — no frameworks, no libraries, no drama:

- **HTML** — for the layout
- **CSS** — for the styling
- **JavaScript** — for making things actually happen

## 💡 Why I Built This

Wanted a hands-on project to practice DOM manipulation and event handling in vanilla JS. Rock Paper Scissors is perfect for that — simple rules, instant feedback, and just enough logic to make it interesting.
